Good to know

How early should I place an order?

For events we recommend at least two to three days notice. Small snack orders can often be prepared the same day, please call to confirm.

Can you make a custom menu?

Yes. Tell us your guest count, budget and preferences and we will plan a menu to suit.

Do you deliver?

We deliver in Dubai on request. Delivery details depend on location and order size, so please ask in your enquiry.
